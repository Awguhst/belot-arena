# Artwork

The lobby illustrations, court figures, player portraits and felt texture were generated for Belot Club with ImageGen. Production ships local WebP assets. The interface draws the playing-card indices, suits and pips so they remain legible at different sizes.

The three seated players use editorial oil portraits. Their names, turn indicators and dealer marker are interface elements. The table overlays the felt with a vignette, fine oval inlays and etched corners that follow the viewport. The spade club mark is a local SVG. Icons use Lucide. PT Serif and Roboto are self-hosted through Fontsource and include Cyrillic glyphs.

## Generation prompts

**Table scene**

Create an ultra-wide premium editorial playing-card still life for an old European card club. Compose a 3:1 landscape banner with uninterrupted deep forest-green wool baize on the left half. Arrange an overlapping fan of vintage ivory French-suited cards on the right. Place a full-colour two-headed king of hearts in front of an ornate ace of spades and a red diamond card. Use antique engraved court artwork, rounded paper corners, soft directional shadows and fine double-gold curved tabletop inlays. Keep the table free of chips and hands. Include only authentic ranks and suits.

**Study illustrations**

Create a 3:1 triptych with three equally sized square panels. Place an antique open teaching book with a spade illustration on moss green, two interlocking vintage jigsaw pieces on smoky indigo, and a small stack of playing cards with a heart pip on dark oxblood. Use gold-olive, pale-periwinkle and copper-rose engraved linework respectively. Centre each complete object with generous margins. Use cohesive intaglio crosshatching and restrained screen-print texture.

**Court portraits**

Create three equal square panels on the same plain warm ivory paper background. Centre a youthful jack in a plumed cap with a halberd, a crowned queen holding a flower and a crowned bearded king with a sceptre. Show each head and shoulders at the same scale. Use conventional flat French-suited playing-card folk art with crisp muted-red, ochre, charcoal and navy woodcut engraving. Leave out ranks, suit symbols, lettering, borders and dividers.
